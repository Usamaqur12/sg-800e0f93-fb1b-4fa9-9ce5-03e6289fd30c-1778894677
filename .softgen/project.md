# dabel ai - Digital Product Marketplace

## Vision
Premium digital product marketplace selling design assets, templates, and creator tools. Target audience: content creators, designers, marketers, and freelancers seeking professional-quality resources.

## Design
Premium dark modern aesthetic with conversion optimization. Professional AI SaaS landing page style.

**Color System:**
- `--background: 216 28% 7%` (deep dark blue)
- `--foreground: 40 56% 98%` (warm white)
- `--card: 217 33% 10%` (elevated dark)
- `--primary: 30 78% 52%` (vibrant amber #E89528)
- `--accent: 30 78% 52%` (amber for CTAs and highlights)
- `--muted: 217 15% 25%` (dark gray)
- `--border: 217 20% 18%` (subtle borders)

**Typography:**
- Display: Newsreader (editorial serif, weights 400, 600, 700)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (professional sans, weights 400, 500, 600, 700)
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono (for technical content)

**Style Direction:**
Premium dark theme with glassmorphism effects, smooth animations, gradient accents, modern card designs, conversion-optimized CTAs, professional trust signals.

## Features
- Conversion-optimized hero with strong CTAs
- Product showcase with 6 digital asset categories
- Featured products with detailed features
- Brand mission and value proposition
- Benefits section with animated cards
- Realistic social proof testimonials
- Professional FAQ and support sections
- Whop.com compliant messaging
- Fully responsive mobile design
