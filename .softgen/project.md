# dabel ai - Digital Product Marketplace

## Vision
Premium digital product marketplace selling design assets, templates, and creator tools. Target audience: content creators, designers, marketers, and freelancers seeking professional-quality resources.

## Design
Editorial sophistication meets creative marketplace polish. Premium digital goods boutique aesthetic.

**Color System:**
- `--primary: 210 45% 15%` (deep navy #1A2332)
- `--background: 40 56% 98%` (warm cream #FAF8F5)
- `--foreground: 210 25% 20%` (dark slate)
- `--accent: 30 78% 48%` (amber #D4831F - for trust/value signals)
- `--muted: 156 20% 55%` (muted sage #7A9B8E)
- `--card: 40 50% 99%` (off-white)
- `--border: 40 20% 88%` (subtle warm gray)

**Typography:**
- Display: Newsreader (editorial serif, weights 400, 600, 700)
- Body: IBM Plex Sans (professional sans, weights 400, 500, 600)
- Mono: IBM Plex Mono (for code/technical content)

**Style Direction:**
Premium editorial with generous whitespace, photography-first product presentation, restrained accent use, sophisticated serif/sans pairing.

## Features
- Homepage with hero section
- Two premium digital products showcased
- Product cards with full details
- Testimonial integration
- Call-to-action buttons
- Responsive design
